About GreenShield Academy

GreenShield Academy is an educational blog dedicated to ethical hacking and cybersecurity. Every guide emphasizes legal, authorized testing — in labs, CTFs, and environments you own or have permission to assess.

Our mission is to make security knowledge accessible through clear explanations, reproducible examples, and responsible use guidelines.

⚠️ Ethical Use Policy

Only test systems you own or have explicit written authorization to assess. Unauthorized access is illegal and harmful.
